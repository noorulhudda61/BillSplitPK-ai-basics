# BillSplitPK - PRD

## What it does
Splits bills equally among friends

## Who it's for
Pakistani students sharing hostel mess and dinner bills

## Features
- Enter bill amount in PKR
- Enter number of friends
- Calculate per person share instantly
- Show total breakdown
- Mobile friendly design

## Future
Easypaisa, JazzCash integration, Urdu language support
